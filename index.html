Guidance

Know what to do

Clear, practical steps — for yourself or someone you're supporting.

In an unsafe moment

  • Move toward people, light and a locked door if you can
  • Call 10111, or send your location to a trusted contact
  • Trust your instincts over politeness

Building a safety plan

  • Identify a safe place and a way to reach it quickly
  • Agree on a code word with someone you trust
  • Keep copies of key documents somewhere separate

Preserving evidence safely

  • Photograph injuries or damage with a timestamp, if safe to do so
  • Keep messages and don't delete them, even upsetting ones
  • Seek medical care — it also creates a record

Supporting someone else

  • Listen without judgement and believe them
  • Let them set the pace on reporting or leaving
  • Help them find professional support when they're ready

Protecting children

  • Teach children the difference between safe and unsafe secrets
  • Take disclosures seriously, however small
  • Contact Childline (0800 055 555) for guidance

How to report

  • Use the form above for a written, private report
  • Call 10111 for anything urgent or ongoing
  • A police report (docket) can support a protection order
